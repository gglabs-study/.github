### About Golden Gate Learning Labs

**Golden Gate Learning Labs (GG Labs)** is an open-source research, engineering, and collaborative learning hub welcoming **undergraduate students**, **independent researchers**, and **industry professionals** across **AI**, **Machine Learning**, **Neuro-Space**, and **Software Engineering** to publish case studies, share datasets, and build verifiable work together.

* **Learn:** Master core algorithms, neural architectures, and modern systems engineering through open lab notes.
* **Build:** Turn theory into deployed full-stack applications, native iOS tools, and reproducible ML pipelines.
* **Prepare:** Document architecture decisions, benchmarks, and competition credentials publicly on GitHub and ORCID.
* **Return:** Bridge career breaks with collaborative engineering sprints and peer-reviewed technical case studies.
* **Grow:** Co-author open datasets, review community pull requests, and lead new interdisciplinary research tracks.

### Open Research & Skillset Tracks

| Track | Focus Areas | Core Stack & Methods |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Artificial Intelligence** | Agentic workflows, local RAG pipelines, and transparent LLM evaluations | `Python` · `FastAPI` · `Vector Search` · `Prompt & Eval Engineering` |
| **Machine Learning** | Quantitative time-series modeling, anomaly detection, and edge inference | `PyTorch` · `Scikit-Learn` · `Core ML` · `GARCH Modeling` |
| **Neuro-Space & Bio-Systems** | Space biology telemetry, closed-loop life support, and biosignal processing | `NASA Open Data` · `GeneLab / POWER` · `Simulation Modeling` |
| **iOS & Full-Stack Cloud** | Native Apple ecosystem architecture and containerized cloud microservices | `Swift` · `SwiftUI` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Docker` |
